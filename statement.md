# VisionDesk - Project Statement

## Problem Statement

Manual estimation of classroom or crowd occupancy is repetitive, time-consuming, and difficult to perform continuously.

VisionDesk uses Computer Vision to automatically detect people from images and videos and convert the detections into occupancy information.

## Scope

The project covers:

- Image-based person detection.
- Video-based person detection.
- Occupancy counting.
- Confidence-based detection.
- Configurable occupancy thresholds.
- Visualization of detected people.
- Video occupancy trends.
- CSV report generation.

The project does not perform personal identification or biometric recognition.

## Target Users

- College administrators
- Classroom coordinators
- Laboratory coordinators
- Event organizers
- Computer Vision students and learners

## High-Level Features

1. Image upload and analysis.
2. Video upload and analysis.
3. Person detection.
4. Bounding-box visualization.
5. Occupancy counting.
6. NORMAL/WARNING/CRITICAL status.
7. Confidence score display.
8. Occupancy trend graph.
9. CSV report generation.
10. Automated testing.

## Expected Output

For image input:

- Annotated image
- Number of people
- Average confidence
- Occupancy status
- Detection table

For video input:

- Annotated video
- Frame-level occupancy
- Peak occupancy
- Occupancy trend
- Downloadable CSV report

## Out of Scope

- Facial recognition
- Individual identification
- Biometric data storage
- Law-enforcement decisions
- Medical or safety certification
